# SpaceFighter Lore

## Overview

The galaxy is divided between two major civilizations:

* **Humans**
* **Jared**

Both control roughly half of the known star systems and have been at war for centuries. The original reason for the conflict has been lost to time. Multiple historical explanations exist, but none can be confirmed.

Beyond the conflict, a third civilization exists:

* **Technicians**

Technicians are rarely seen and operate mainly around **hidden gates** and ancient infrastructure. Many citizens believe they are myths.

---

# The Human–Jared War

The war likely began during the early era of interstellar expansion when two rival corporations developed different strategies to build a galactic transport network.

Possible causes include:

* disputes over warp gate construction priority
* chaos energy reactor accidents
* corporate espionage
* destruction of a Jared hatchery
* automated defense systems misidentifying fleets as hostile

Over centuries the conflict became cultural rather than strategic. Today, most participants no longer know why it started.

The war persists largely because political and military systems depend on it.

---

# Civilizations

## Humans

Humans dominate stable star systems connected through **warp gates**.

Their society values:

* engineering precision
* structural reliability
* preservation of the "human standard"

Human technology emphasizes:

* strong ship hulls
* powerful shields
* large CPU capacity
* modular ship platforms

Human ships tend to be stronger and more durable than those of other factions.

However, human culture is often described as emotionally distant and procedural.

---

## Jared

Jared are a humanoid species with a strong communal culture.

Biological traits:

* egg reproduction
* crystal-based energy systems
* chaos-energy technology

Cultural traits:

* pack-like social bonds
* strong loyalty
* curiosity and emotional expression

Jared ships are typically faster and more frail, but their **outfits and weapons are extremely powerful**.

Their technology often uses chaos energy aligned through crystals.

---

## Technicians

Technicians are extremely advanced but rarely intervene in galactic affairs.

Their technology focuses on:

* stealth
* teleportation
* sensor networks
* infrastructure maintenance

Most Technician technology is **non-combat oriented**, reinforcing their philosophy of avoiding war.

Occasionally they deploy defensive ships to repel pirates or protect hidden gates.

---

# The Singularity

The only known true Technician warship is the **Singularity-class vessel**.

Characteristics:

* extremely high energy capacity
* massive outfit space and CPU
* exotic spacetime-based weapons

One known system mounted on this ship is capable of creating a **temporary artificial black hole**, pulling nearby ships into its gravity well.

Singularity ships are extremely rare and appear only during major crises.

---

# Free Worlds

Hidden throughout the galaxy are systems known as **Free Worlds**.

These systems are connected through **hidden gates** and are protected by a group called the **Young Technicians**.

In Free Worlds:

* Humans
* Jared
* Technicians

live peacefully together.

These societies use hybrid technologies combining the strengths of each civilization.

Free Worlds demonstrate that coexistence between the species is possible.

---

# Peace Gates

Some peripheral star systems contain a special type of hidden gate.

These gates only activate for ships **without weapons equipped**.

This mechanism prevents military fleets from entering Free World space and acts as a cultural filter ensuring only peaceful travelers can pass.

The existence of these gates is mostly unknown. Knowledge about them spreads mainly through rumors among pilots.

---

# Rumors and Discovery

Pilots occasionally report strange encounters with unusual gates.

One story tells of a human pilot fleeing pirates whose weapons had been disabled during battle. He accidentally entered one of these gates and encountered a Technician patrol.

The patrol created another gate and returned him to the **Sol system**, preventing him from discovering what lay beyond.

---

# Ancient Expansion Program

Archaeological discoveries suggest that both Humans and Jared were originally part of a **joint expansion project**.

Roles were complementary:

| Species | Intended Role              |
| ------- | -------------------------- |
| Humans  | stable warp infrastructure |
| Jared   | chaos-space exploration    |

Together they were meant to build the interstellar transportation network.

---

# The Hidden Truth

Further discoveries reveal deeper origins.

* Modern Humans are descendants of **bio-engineered humanoid android colonists** launched in generational ships to build the warp network.
* Jared were an engineered **companion species** designed to assist those colonists and operate in unstable environments.
* The **Technicians** are the descendants of the original humans who survived a nuclear war on Earth and later re-emerged after millennia of adaptation.

Technicians now observe the galaxy and avoid intervention, believing civilizations must learn to avoid repeating past mistakes.

---

# Thematic Structure

Each civilization represents a different fragment of humanity:

| Civilization | Represents                        |
| ------------ | --------------------------------- |
| Humans       | structure and legacy              |
| Jared        | curiosity and social bonds        |
| Technicians  | wisdom gained through catastrophe |

The war between Humans and Jared is therefore a tragic misunderstanding between two civilizations originally designed to cooperate.

---

# Story Structure

The game narrative unfolds in four chapters.

## Early Game - The Frontier

Players experience frontier life and the Human–Jared conflict.

## Mid Game - The past

Exploration of ruins and archaeological discoveries hint at ancient cooperation.

## Late Game - Contact with Tecnicians

Technician systems and hidden gates reveal the deeper truth about the civilizations.

## Endgame - Choose the destiny

The player learns the origin of the species and must decide the future of the galaxy.

Possible outcomes include:

* revealing the truth and pursuing peace
* supporting one faction’s dominance
* protecting the neutrality of the Free Worlds
* rebuilding the ancient expansion network.

---

# Core Theme

The original cause of the war no longer matters.

The true question facing the galaxy is whether its civilizations will continue repeating ancient mistakes or rediscover the cooperation they were originally created for.
