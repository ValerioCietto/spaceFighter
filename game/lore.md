# SpaceFighter Lore

## The Divided Galaxy

The known galaxy is split between two major civilizations:

- **Human Coalition**
- **Jared Dominion**

Each controls roughly half of known star systems. Between them lies a turbulent frontier known as **the Shatter Belt**, where patrol fleets clash, mercenaries thrive, and abandoned infrastructure drifts through forgotten systems.

The war between Humans and Jared has lasted for centuries. Its original cause is unclear. Official histories give different explanations: territorial disputes, ideological clashes, or early first-contact misunderstandings.

Most citizens no longer question it.

War has simply become the natural state of the galaxy.

---

## Humans

Modern Humans dominate vast regions of stable space connected through **Warp Gates**.

Human society values:

- order
- stability
- technological precision
- preservation of genetic purity

Human governments maintain strict control over medical technologies and genomic stability. Regular **genome calibration scans** are common practice across human worlds.

Many citizens view this as a sign of advanced medicine.

Others see it as an obsession with preserving something ancient.

Human engineers remain masters of **structured technologies** such as:

- warp navigation
- beam weapons
- kinetic weapon systems
- large-scale infrastructure

Human culture often appears cold and procedural to outsiders.

But Humans believe discipline is the foundation of civilization.

---

## The Jared

The **Jared Dominion** occupies large regions of chaotic stellar environments where unstable energy phenomena are common.

Unlike Humans, Jared society revolves around **clans and packs**.

Their culture values:

- loyalty
- curiosity
- strong social bonds
- courage in adversity

Jared reproduce through **eggs**, which they proudly consider a natural and civilized method of creating new generations.

Jared technology relies heavily on **crystal energy systems** that can align and stabilize chaotic energy fields.

This allows them to exploit environments that Humans consider extremely dangerous.

Typical Jared technologies include:

- crystal resonance weapons
- chaos-energy propulsion
- plasma ignition systems

Many Jared myths describe a time when their ancestors traveled alongside another species that "built calm roads between stars".

Historians consider these stories symbolic.

---

## The Technicians

Rare and mysterious sightings across the galaxy describe a third civilization known as **the Technicians**.

Encounters are extremely uncommon.

Technician vessels are reported to possess:

- advanced stealth technology
- teleportation capabilities
- unknown energy systems
- extremely precise weapons

They are most frequently seen near **Hidden Gates**, a class of ancient gateway structures that cannot be activated by Human or Jared technology.

Because Technician sightings are so rare, many scholars debate whether they exist at all.

Some theories claim they are:

- automated remnants of an extinct civilization
- custodians of ancient infrastructure
- observers studying galactic development

Technicians almost never intervene in conflicts between Humans and Jared.

When they appear, they observe silently and then disappear.

---

## The Gate Network

Interstellar travel depends on three types of gateways.

### Warp Gates

Stable and widely used by Humans.

Warp Gates form the backbone of interstellar commerce and military logistics.

They allow reliable travel between star systems through controlled spatial corridors.

---

### Chaos Gates

These rare gateways connect systems through unstable energy channels.

Jared technologies can sometimes stabilize these pathways using crystal resonance.

Travel through Chaos Gates is considered dangerous.

---

### Hidden Gates

The rarest type of gateway.

Hidden Gates appear dormant and invisible to most scanning systems.

Only Technician vessels have been observed activating them.

Their true purpose remains unknown.

---

## Cultural Irony

Despite centuries of war, Humans and Jared share several unexpected similarities:

- similar humanoid body structures
- compatible technologies in certain ancient ruins
- references in early archaeological records suggesting cooperation

Most historians dismiss these findings as coincidence.

Yet the deeper the galaxy is explored, the more fragments of a forgotten history emerge.

---

## Rumors Among Pilots

Independent pilots and explorers report unusual discoveries across remote systems:

- ancient colony ships containing humanoid incubation chambers
- ruined hatcheries built with both crystal and mechanical technologies
- warp gates with interfaces compatible with multiple species
- stealth vessels silently observing battlefields

Most of these stories remain unverified.

But some explorers believe the truth about the galaxy's past is hidden in the deepest ruins of the gate network.


---

## The Player

The player begins as a small independent pilot with a modest ship and limited reputation.

Through exploration, trade, combat, and discovery, they gradually uncover fragments of a much older story.

A story that predates the Human–Jared war.

A story that may reveal why the galaxy was connected in the first place.

And why some civilizations prefer to remain hidden.
